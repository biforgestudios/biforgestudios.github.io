Project AGI Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Project AGI does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

This Privacy Policy applies to the game Project AGI, package name com.biforgestudios.projectagi, developed and published by BiForge Studios.

Information collection

Project AGI does not require an account and does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, advertising data, or diagnostic telemetry. The game does not contain advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

Local game data

Game progress, save files, and settings such as volume, reduced motion, and vibration preferences are stored locally on the player's device. BiForge Studios cannot access this data. Players can remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

Device permissions

Project AGI may use the Android vibration permission to provide optional haptic feedback when the player makes a selection. This permission does not provide access to personal data. Vibration can be disabled in the game's Settings menu.

Third-party services

The game itself does not transmit information to third-party services. Google Play may process information related to downloading, purchasing, installing, or updating the game under Google's own privacy policies. That processing is operated by Google and is not controlled by Project AGI.

Children's privacy

Project AGI is not designed specifically for children. Because the game does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Project AGI adds online features, analytics, advertising, accounts, or another feature that changes how data is handled, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to biforgestudios@gmail.com.
