Privacy Policy

Loopmetric
Developer: BiForge Studios
Android package: com.biforgestudios.loopmetric
Effective date and last updated: 15 October 2026

Overview

Loopmetric is an offline geometric puzzle game by BiForge Studios. This policy describes the current game and this privacy-policy webpage. The game does not require an account or login.

Data handled by the game

Loopmetric does not send personal information or gameplay data to BiForge Studios. The game does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, social sign-in, online leaderboards or a developer-operated cloud service. We do not sell or share personal data collected through the game.

The game does not request Internet access or permission to access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or personal photos. Optional sounds and gentle haptic feedback run on your device.

Data stored on your device

Loopmetric saves information in its private app storage so you can continue playing. This includes your current board and pieces, scores, unlocked shapes, progression and mastery, daily-puzzle attempts and results, tutorial completion, selected language, and music, sound and vibration settings.

These records are used to provide the game's features. They are not uploaded to BiForge Studios. The current game does not provide account-based synchronisation or cloud saves. Its local recovery copy is also stored on your device.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes your saved progress and preferences. BiForge Studios cannot recover your local saves or delete them remotely. There is no game account or developer-hosted player database to delete.

Google Play purchases and platform services

Purchases, downloads and distribution through Google Play are handled by Google. Loopmetric does not collect payment-card details or include an in-game payment system. Google may process account, transaction, device and diagnostic information for its own platform services under its own policies. Information Google makes available to developers, such as order details or technical reports, may be used to provide support and maintain the game.

For information about Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the email address and any name, message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy questions. Please do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of support information, subject to any retention requirements. Email is handled through our email provider, Google.

Age group

Loopmetric is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. The game's data practices described above apply to all players.

This website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. We have not added analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to this page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for the hosting provider's practices.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date if the game's features or data practices change. This policy describes the current release; it does not authorise additional data collection in future versions without appropriate disclosures.
