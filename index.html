Privacy Policy

Zero Luck Arcade
Developer: BiForge Studios
Last updated: 30 July 2026

Overview

Zero Luck Arcade is an offline arcade game developed by BiForge Studios. The app does not require an account, login, subscription, or network connection.

Data collection and sharing

Zero Luck Arcade does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, sensitive information, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or advertising data.

The app does not use advertising services, analytics services, cloud storage, social sign-in, or third-party tracking technologies.

Locally stored data

The app stores game records and settings locally on the user's device. This may include:

  • High scores and game records
  • Vibration and audio preferences

This information remains on the device and is not transmitted to BiForge Studios or any third party.

Permissions

The app may use the device's vibration function to provide optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, files, or other sensitive device information.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored records remain on the device until the user resets the records, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Because BiForge Studios does not receive or store this information, there is no server-side user data to retain or delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. No personal information is collected from any user.

Security

Since the app does not transmit or remotely store user data, there is no remote user-data database associated with Zero Luck Arcade. Users should still protect access to their own device using appropriate device security.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page, and the date above will be updated.
