Adagia Support

Adagia – A Better Perspective · BiForge Studios

Need help, found an incorrect quotation, or want to suggest an improvement?

Email biforgestudios@gmail.com

For a technical issue, please include your app version from Settings, your device model, and what happened. For a content issue, include the perspective title and thinker. Only send screenshots or personal information you are comfortable sharing.

Saved perspectives

Saved cards are stored in the app on your device. A device backup may restore them; Adagia has no account-based sync. Cards under editorial review keep their saved IDs and may return if approved.

Adagia Full purchases

In purchase-enabled versions, open Settings → Adagia Full → Restore purchases while online and signed into the same app-store account used for the purchase. Pending payments unlock the full library only after confirmation. Your saved cards are kept if access changes.

For purchase support or a request to access or delete purchase-related personal data, email us with your app version and store order reference if available. Do not send card numbers or passwords. Clearing app data does not refund a purchase or remove store records. Refunds are handled through your app store.

Sources and sharing

Reading cards works offline. Opening a source or the privacy website requires a browser and usually an internet connection. Sharing uses your device’s system sharing menu.
