Adagia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Your saved perspectives stay in app storage. Adagia does not send them to BiForge Studios. Purchase-enabled versions use RevenueCat for purchase verification and restoration; reading preferences stay local.

About this policy

This policy applies to Adagia – A Better Perspective, developed by BiForge Studios, including the Android app studio.biforge.adagia. Adagia provides philosophical perspectives and works without an account or an internet connection for its core features.

Information handled by the app

Adagia does not send your saved perspectives, selected mood or usage history to BiForge Studios. The app has no advertising or general reading-activity analytics. In purchase-enabled versions, RevenueCat and your app store process purchase information to provide and restore Adagia Full. There is no Adagia account.

Data stored on your device

Saved perspective IDs, onboarding completion and your haptic-feedback preference are stored in local app preferences. The selected mood and recent-card history are kept in memory for the current app session. Sharing a perspective creates a temporary image in the app cache. In purchase-enabled versions, the purchase SDK also caches an anonymous customer identifier and purchase-access status on your device.

Purchases and RevenueCat

In purchase-enabled versions, RevenueCat connects when the app starts to check purchase access, including for users of the free library. It processes an anonymous app-specific customer identifier, store receipts or purchase tokens, purchased products, transaction history, access status and technical connection information needed to operate the service. BiForge Studios can access purchase records and purchase-related reports in RevenueCat. This is used to verify purchases, restore access, handle refunds and understand sales. Your mood, saved list, card selections and reflections are not sent to RevenueCat. Advertising identifier collection and optional SDK diagnostics are disabled. Your payment details are handled by the app store; Adagia does not receive your card number. Builds without purchase configuration do not connect to RevenueCat.

Purchase records and your choices

Purchase records are retained by our purchase providers to maintain access, prevent fraud and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Clearing local app data does not delete store or RevenueCat records or refund a purchase. Contact biforgestudios@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of purchase-related personal data; we may need your store order reference to locate and verify the request. We will explain any records that must be retained. Store records and refunds are also managed under your app store’s policies. RevenueCat may process information outside your country; see its privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy for its processing and safeguards.

Device backup and transfer

Your operating system may include local app preferences in device backups or transfers, depending on your device and backup settings. This can restore saved perspectives and preferences after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. Backups are managed by your operating-system or backup provider, not by BiForge Studios. Manage them through your device or provider settings; removing local app data does not necessarily remove an existing backup.

Sharing and external links

Nothing is shared automatically. When you choose Share, the text or image is passed to the app or recipient you select through the system sharing menu. Opening a source, privacy page or app-store link opens an external service, which may receive normal connection information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. Adagia does not attach your mood, saved list or recent-card history to those links.

Support messages

If you choose to email BiForge Studios, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to answer your request and resolve the reported issue, and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not needed for support.

Retention and deletion

Remove an individual saved card with Save/Saved, or use Settings → Clear saved perspectives to remove the saved list on this device. Clear the app storage through your device settings to remove local preferences as well. Uninstalling removes local app storage, but an operating-system backup may be retained or restored separately. Temporary share images may be removed by clearing the app cache; copies already sent to other apps or recipients are governed by their services. BiForge Studios has no server-side copy of your saved perspectives to delete.

Permissions and security

The app uses optional haptic feedback and user-initiated system sharing and external links. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location. Protect local data with your device lock and manage access to device backups. RevenueCat maintains purchase records for purchase-enabled versions; reading preferences remain local.

Children

Adagia is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask users to provide their age. Purchase-enabled versions process the purchase information described above. If a child has sent personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

This website

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. BiForge Studios has not added analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to it. GitHub may process technical request information to operate and secure its hosting service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Updates and contact

We will update this policy when Adagia’s data practices change. The current policy and its revision date are available on the website and in the app. For privacy questions, support or deletion requests, contact biforgestudios@gmail.com.
